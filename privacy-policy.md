PRIVACY POLICY — INTEGRAL STUDENT PORTAL

Last updated: 17 October 2026

1. INTRODUCTION

Integral Student Portal ("the App") provides students with convenient access to academic and student-related information. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be accessed, processed, used, and protected when you use the App.

2. INFORMATION WE HANDLE

Depending on the features you use and the university systems connected to the App, information may include:

• Account and login information, such as student ID or roll number and authentication credentials.
• Student profile information, such as name, course, academic details, and contact information.
• Academic information, such as attendance records, examination results, and related student records.
• Fee-related information, where available through the connected student portal.
• Technical information required for the App to function, troubleshoot errors, and maintain security.

The exact information processed depends on the services enabled in the App and the connected university systems.

3. HOW INFORMATION IS USED

Information is used to:
• Authenticate students and provide access to the student portal.
• Display relevant academic, attendance, profile, and fee information.
• Maintain the security and reliability of the App.
• Diagnose technical issues and improve functionality.
• Respond to privacy or support requests.

4. DATA SOURCES AND SHARING

The App may retrieve student information from the university's student portal or associated backend services. Information may be processed by the university and the technical service providers necessary to operate the App.

Information is not sold by the App developer. Information is not shared with third parties for their independent marketing purposes unless this is separately disclosed and legally permitted.

Before publication, the developer must verify the actual backend services, analytics, crash-reporting tools, and other SDKs used by the App and update this section accordingly.

5. DATA SECURITY

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. Information transmitted over networks should be protected using HTTPS/TLS where supported. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

Student academic records may be retained by the university according to its applicable policies and legal obligations. The App developer should retain information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

For requests concerning university records, contact the university through its official support channels. For information handled by the App developer, contact the privacy contact listed below.

If the App supports account creation, users must have an accessible method to request account deletion, including through an appropriate web resource where required by Google Play. Applicable user data must be deleted in accordance with the relevant policy and legal requirements.

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The App is intended for students who are authorised to use the university's student services. The developer should ensure that any collection of personal information from children complies with applicable laws and Google Play's Families policies where relevant.

8. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The App may rely on university systems, hosting providers, authentication services, or other technical services. These services may process information as necessary to provide their functionality. Their actual involvement and data practices must be verified before this policy is published.

9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version and its effective date will be made available at the published privacy policy URL.
