# Independent app: brand colors yes, official marks never

The app is an independent client and is not affiliated with Integral University. It uses the university's brand colors, but never its official logos, seals, emblems or accreditation badges (the ICPE emblem, the round seal, the NAAC A+ badge), whether in the UI, the app icon, the splash screen or the store listing. Using those marks could make the app look official, which risks store removal under impersonation policies. The app's own icon is its only mark, and the login screen and Profile show an "Independent app · Not affiliated with Integral University" line.


Update (2026-10-22): the app no longer uses the university's colors either. It has its own five-color palette (see DESIGN.md › Color). The rule against official marks is unchanged.
